**Theorem [T-0408](../theorems/T-0408.md).** Assume independent rolls.

(a) Greed: the number of real-chest claims (while not vulnerable) until the first Shield is geometric with $p = 1/4$: mean 4, variance 12. At least one Shield in $n$ claims has probability $1 - (3/4)^n$: $7/16$ for 2 claims, $37/64$ for 3 (the first $n$ above one half), and at least $9/10$ from $n = 9$. Under [U-0511](../unknowns.md#u-0511) reading B (each Shield adds 20 min), a claim is worth 5 Shield minutes in expectation. Under readings A (refresh) and C (ignored) overlapping Shields waste time, and under reading D (independent Shields coexist) two Shields active at once protect no more than one, so in those three readings 5 minutes per claim is an upper bound on protected time. In every reading a Vulnerability Dart removes the Shield early ([M-0215](../rules/items.md#m-0215)), which can only lower the figure further.

(b) Trolling, one 3% roll per package ([U-0540](../unknowns.md#u-0540) reading A): packages until the first diversion are geometric with $p = 3/100$: mean $100/3 \approx 33.3$, variance $9700/9 \approx 1077.8$; a better-than-even chance of at least one diversion needs 23 packages, and $9/10$ needs 76. If the 5 packages of an emergency airdrop ([M-0418](../rules/viewer.md#m-0418)) each roll ([U-0411](../unknowns.md#u-0411) readings A or B), at least one is diverted with probability $1 - (97/100)^5 \approx 0.1413$. If instead each of $N$ Trolling contestants rolls separately ([U-0540](../unknowns.md#u-0540) reading C), a package is diverted with probability $1 - (97/100)^N$ ($591/10000$ for $N = 2$).

*Proof.* Geometric and binomial laws as in [T-0407](../theorems/T-0407.md); the thresholds are the smallest $n$ with $1 - (1-p)^n \ge 1/2$ or $\ge 9/10$, found by exact computation. Under reading B of [U-0511](../unknowns.md#u-0511) the Shield time is $20$ min times a binomial count, mean $20/4 = 5$ min per claim; overlap can only reduce it. $\square$

*Label:* (a) the geometric and binomial results `CONDITIONAL` on Q4.1 (independent rolls) only; the Shield minutes also on [U-0511](../unknowns.md#u-0511) (each reading stated); the time to a Shield is not computable yet, since how often real chests appear is unknown ([U-0015](../unknowns.md#u-0015)). (b) `CONDITIONAL` on Q4.1, [U-0540](../unknowns.md#u-0540) and [U-0411](../unknowns.md#u-0411), with [C-0403](../unknowns.md#c-0403) and [C-0501](../unknowns.md#c-0501) open. *Test:* `test_t0408_greed_shield_and_trolling_diversion`.
