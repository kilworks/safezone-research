## Wayback 20261008185557: first capture

```
Introduction
Safe Zone is an event hosted by Ice Poseidon, any Kick streamer is welcome to join; show up to Austin, Texas and compete to win $50,000.
The objective is simple: don't die, stay in the safezone, and endure. The Safe Zone will move in whatever unpredictable way Ice Poseidon chooses, it can change in size or speed, or even split apart.
Things may be changed, always check for the latest information.
[#damage]
Damage
After you leave a safe zone, you get a grace period of 5 s. Then you lose 1 HP every 2 s: 30 HP per minute.
The Trolling sigil can spawn an unsafe circle inside a safe zone.
See Damage and protection for what blocks circle damage.
If the event is paused, all damage and actions are stopped until it resumes.
[#hp]
HP
Contestants start with 100 HP. At 0 HP you are eliminated. Some sigils raise or lower your max HP.
[#inventory]
Inventory and reach
You carry up to 3 items. Items do not stack.
Most actions need you to be within 5 m of the target.
Trade items with other contestants. A trade can hold up to 10 items.
[#deaths]
Deaths and gravestones
When you die, a gravestone with your items appears where you were last seen. Other contestants can loot it.
An eliminated contestant can sometimes be revived within 10 min of their death.
Deaths and revives
```
