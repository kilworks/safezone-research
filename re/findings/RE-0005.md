# RE-0005: snapshot history (Wayback), kept as history only

Captured: 2026-11-11. Label: `DOCUMENTED` for the archive inventory; no rule is taken from it.

## Question

Between snapshots, what changed?

## Method

`tools/wayback.py`: CDX query per page (status 200, collapsed by digest), one `id_` capture per digest, 5 s between requests. `tools/diff_snapshots.py` diffs the rendered text of consecutive captures into `corpus/diffs/`.

## Result

- Archived pages: `/safezone` (15 distinct captures, 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-22) and `/safezone/wiki` (7, 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-20). The 18 other wiki pages and `/safezone/wiki/chests` have no capture.
- The captures describe the October (Austin) edition. Text changes are listed in `corpus/diffs/overview.md` and `corpus/diffs/wiki.md`. Result blocks (placements, statistics, clips) are cut there because they name contestants.
- Bounded negative: the client scripts of October were not fetched, so `compare_application_versions` was not run. Searched: CDX for the 20 wiki page URLs only.

## Decision

The October rules are obsolete. Only the 2026-11-11 snapshot is a source for the knowledge base, the theory and the simulator (project owner, 2026-11-11). `tools/check_kb.py` rejects any source with another snapshot date.
