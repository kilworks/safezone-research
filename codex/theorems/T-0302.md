# T-0302

Extracted from [theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md](../../theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0302](../theorems/T-0302.md).** Let a Piety contestant with missing HP $m$ hold a potion of tier conversion value $c$. Drinking earns at least $c$ tokens, whatever remainder the token counter carries, if and only if the token basis is nominal ([U-0501](../unknowns.md#u-0501) B or C), or it is effective (A or D) and $m \ge 5c$, that is 5, 10, 15 or 20 missing HP for the small, medium, large or gigantic tier. At full deficit drinking earns 1, 2, 4, 9 tokens (bases A, B) or 1, 2, 4, 8 (C, D), against 1, 2, 3, 4 for converting.

*Proof.* A drink adds $q$ to the counter, with $q = \min(m, \lfloor 1.15x \rfloor)$ (A), $\lfloor 1.15x \rfloor$ (B), $x$ (C) or $\min(m, x)$ (D). With carried remainder $R \in \{0, \dots, 4\}$ the drink earns $\lfloor (R + q)/5 \rfloor - \lfloor R/5 \rfloor = \lfloor (R+q)/5 \rfloor \ge \lfloor q/5 \rfloor$, with equality at $R = 0$ (which is also the per-event reading [U-0500](../unknowns.md#u-0500) B). So drinking earns at least $c$ for every carry iff $\lfloor q/5 \rfloor \ge c$, iff $q \ge 5c$. Nominal bases: $\lfloor 1.15x \rfloor \ge x$ and $x = 5, 10, 20, 40 \ge 5c = 5, 10, 15, 20$. Effective bases: $\min(m, \cdot) \ge 5c$ iff $m \ge 5c$, since both caps ($x$ and $\lfloor 1.15x \rfloor$) are at least $5c$. $\square$

*Consequence.* Under every reading, once the contestant is missing at least $5c$ HP a drink gives at least as many tokens as a sale and the HP on top; for the large and gigantic tiers at a large deficit it gives strictly more tokens (4 against 3, 8 or 9 against 4). Selling only pays in tokens when the contestant is nearly full and the counter uses effective HP. The theorem compares one potion at one moment: a potion kept is also insurance and can be broken by a zombie ([M-0443](../rules/zombies.md#m-0443)), which the comparison leaves out.

**HP per slot.** All Piety health potions share one slot, up to 6 ([M-0506](../rules/items.md#m-0506)): at most $6 \times 46 = 276$ HP in one slot, more than any reachable Piety max HP (220), or $6 \times 4 = 24$ tokens if converted. For other sigils one potion per slot under [U-0200](../unknowns.md#u-0200) A (at most 40 HP; 20 for Protection); under [U-0200](../unknowns.md#u-0200) B the stack size is unknown.

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0500](../unknowns.md#u-0500) and [U-0501](../unknowns.md#u-0501) (the theorem covers all their readings), and on [M-0501](../rules/economy.md#m-0501) counting potion heals (it names "any source except revives"). *Test:* `test_t0302_piety_drink_or_convert` (every Piety max HP, every missing HP, every tier, basis and carry).
