**Theorem [T-0405](../theorems/T-0405.md).** Under [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004) reading A and [U-0203](../unknowns.md#u-0203) reading A, let $J = \lfloor (h - 1)/\ell \rfloor$ be the number of failures a contestant at $h$ survives, and suppose one success is enough after any of them: $\min(M, h - j\ell + g) \ge k$ for $j = 0, \dots, \min(J, n-1)$. Then with $m = \min(n, J + 1)$:

(a) the probability of reaching $k$ is $1 - (9/10)^m$;
(b) the number of drinks $D$ used satisfies $\Pr(D = i) = (9/10)^{i-1}/10$ for $i < m$ and $\Pr(D = m) = (9/10)^{m-1}$, so $\mathbb{E}[D] = 10\,(1 - (9/10)^m)$;
(c) under reading C (no death; HP after $j$ failures is $\max(h - j\ell, 1)$, and the hypothesis is read with that value), $J$ is unbounded: $m = n$, and with unlimited potions $D$ is geometric with mean 10 and variance 90.

Example at $M = 100$, $h = 40$, $k = 60$: $J = 1$ (40 then 10 HP), and 10 + 50 = 60 reaches $k$, so with two or more potions the chance is $19/100$. Separately, for reachable $M$ a contestant at full HP has $J = 3$: the fourth failure in a row kills, so at most 4 drinks can be taken before death under reading A (used in [T-0406](../theorems/T-0406.md)). The closed form above does not apply at full HP, since it needs $h < k \le M$.

*Proof.* Each drink is an independent trial with success probability $1/10$. Under the hypothesis, the first success reaches $k$; before it, the $j$-th failure leaves $h - j\ell$, alive iff $j \le J$. The process stops at the first success, at death (failure number $J + 1$) or when the $n$ potions are gone, so at most $m$ trials happen and success has probability $1 - (9/10)^m$. The law of $D$ is that of a geometric variable truncated at $m$; $\mathbb{E}[D] = \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} (9/10)^i$. $J = 3$ at $h = M$ because $3 \cdot 3M/10 \le M - 1 < 4 \cdot 3M/10$ for $M \ge 10$, with $\ell = 3M/10$ for reachable $M$ ([T-0400](../theorems/T-0400.md)(b)). $\square$

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004) (reading A for (a), (b); C for (c)), [U-0203](../unknowns.md#u-0203) reading A, Q4.1 (independent rolls) and Q4.2 (back-to-back drinks, no cooldown). The value $J = 3$ at full HP also uses [T-0400](../theorems/T-0400.md)(b). *Test:* `test_t0405_repeated_drinks_toward_a_target` (dynamic programming over every reachable $M$, every third $h$, every fourth $k$, $n \le 6$, both readings, against the closed form), `test_t0405_examples`.
