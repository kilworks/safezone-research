# T-0306

Extracted from [theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md](../../theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0306](../theorems/T-0306.md).** (a) Under [U-0007](../unknowns.md#u-0007) reading B with ticks reset, a Healing contestant who takes damage at least once every 1,799 s never regenerates after the first hit; at exactly 1,800 s the result depends on the same-second order (the hit first: no regeneration). Every documented periodic damage source ([T-0305](../theorems/T-0305.md) list) qualifies while it lasts. With ticks do not reset, a periodic source does not stop regeneration at all, and only discrete hits count.

(b) Under [U-0007](../unknowns.md#u-0007) reading A (fixed clock), regeneration is slower than every documented periodic damage, so by [T-0305](../theorems/T-0305.md) it holds none of them off. Against bleeding alone (1 HP every 300 s, first tick 300 s after the hit, [U-0332](../unknowns.md#u-0332) A), from 100 of 100 HP, death comes between 35,700 s and 36,000 s (9 h 55 min to 10 h) over every phase of the regeneration clock and both same-second orders, against 30,000 s (8 h 20 min) without regeneration. Under reading B with ticks reset, bleeding suppresses regeneration and death comes at 30,000 s. Under reading B with ticks do not reset, the Deep Cut hit at second 0 is the last reset, regeneration runs at 1,800, 3,600, ... s, and death comes at 35,700 s.

*Proof.* (a) The timer reaches 1,800 s only after 1,800 s without a reset. (b) $1/1800 < 1/300$; the death times are computed exactly for each of the 1,800 phases and both orders (event-driven, integer seconds), and lie inside the bounds of [T-0305](../theorems/T-0305.md) part 2, $[30000, 36720]$. Reading B with ticks do not reset is reading A with phase 1,800. $\square$

*Consequence.* Regeneration helps only between fights, at 48 HP a day at most ([T-0011](../theorems/T-0011.md)). A Healing contestant who can heal herself ([U-0623](../unknowns.md#u-0623) reading C) cures her own bleeding and poison every 10 min, which matters far more than regeneration. Where the regeneration clock starts (event start or sigil pick) is open ([M-0601](../rules/sigil.md#m-0601)).

*Label:* (a) `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0007](../unknowns.md#u-0007) reading B and on the ticks-reset reading ([U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002), [U-0332](../unknowns.md#u-0332)), each case named; (b) `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0332](../unknowns.md#u-0332) reading A and on [U-0007](../unknowns.md#u-0007) reading A, or on reading B with the tick reading named. *Test:* `test_t0306_regeneration_extended` (both tick readings).
