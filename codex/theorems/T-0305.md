# T-0305

Extracted from [theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md](../../theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0305](../theorems/T-0305.md).**
1. If $r \ge \lambda$ and $h > a + c - g$, the contestant never dies, and HP never falls below $h - (a + c - g)$.
2. If $r < \lambda$, the contestant dies at a time $\tau$ with
$$
\frac{hb}{a} \le \tau \le \frac{h + a + c}{\lambda - r}.
$$

So the largest steady damage rate a renewable heal can hold off forever is its own rate $r$, with no exception at equality.

*Proof.* (1) Fix a moment just after a hit (HP is lowest just after hits). Let $t_0$ be the last earlier moment at which a heal was capped, so HP was $M \ge h$ then, or $t_0 = 0$ with HP $h$ if no heal was ever capped. Since $t_0$ nothing was capped, so HP now is the start value plus $\Delta = cH - aD$, where $H$ and $D$ count heals and hits since $t_0$, and $L = t - t_0$. Three cases. From the start: $D \le \lfloor t/b \rfloor \le \lambda t$ and $H \ge t/e - 1$ (as $\varphi \le e$), so $\Delta \ge (r - \lambda)t - c > -(a + c)$. After a capped heal, hit first in shared seconds: a hit at $t_0$ came before the heal and is not counted, so the hits counted are those at $t, t - b, t - 2b, \dots$ that lie after $t_0$, and $D = \lceil L/b \rceil < L/b + 1$ (this is not $\le L/b$ in general: $b = 10$, a capped heal at $t_0 = 5$ and a hit at $t = 10$ give $D = 1 > L/b = 1/2$); the heals counted lie strictly between $t_0$ and $t$, so $H \ge \lceil L/e \rceil - 1 \ge L/e - 1$; hence $\Delta > c(L/e - 1) - a(L/b + 1) = (r - \lambda)L - a - c \ge -(a+c)$. After a capped heal, heal first: $D \le \lfloor L/b \rfloor + 1$ and $H = \lfloor L/e \rfloor > L/e - 1$, so $\Delta > (r - \lambda)L - a - c \ge -(a + c)$. In every case $\Delta > -(a + c)$, and $\Delta$ is a multiple of $g$, so $\Delta \ge -(a + c) + g$. HP is therefore at least $h - (a + c - g) \ge 1$. (2) Heals never lower HP, so HP $\ge h - a\lfloor t/b \rfloor$ and death needs $a \lfloor t/b \rfloor \ge h$, so $t \ge hb/a$. On the other side the total healed by time $t$ is at most $c\,(\lfloor (t - \varphi)/e \rfloor + 1) < rt + c$ and the damage is $a \lfloor t/b \rfloor > \lambda t - a$, so HP $< h + a + c - (\lambda - r)t$, which is at most 0 once $t \ge (h + a + c)/(\lambda - r)$. $\square$

*Tightness.* In the test domain ($a, c \le 3$; $b, e \le 6$; $M \le 9$; both orders), the dip $a + c - g$ is reached, and a contestant starting at exactly $h = a + c - g$ can die, for every pair $(a, c)$. Dropping $+c$ from the upper bound of part 2 makes it false: the test finds cases that die after $(h + a)/(\lambda - r)$. Whether the dip is reached for every $a$ and $c$ is not claimed.

*Corollary (several heal sources).* Replace the single heal by sources $j = 1, \dots, k$, each healing $c_j$ HP every $e_j$ s from a phase $1 \le \varphi_j \le e_j$, with any order inside a shared second. Write $r = \sum_j c_j/e_j$, $C = \sum_j c_j$ and $g = \gcd(a, c_1, \dots, c_k)$. If $r \ge \lambda$ and $h > a + C - g$ the contestant never dies and HP never falls below $h - (a + C - g)$; if $r < \lambda$ death comes at a time $\tau$ with $hb/a \le \tau \le (h + a + C)/(\lambda - r)$. *Proof.* The proof of the theorem, with one count $H_j$ per source: the source that was capped at $t_0$ obeys the bounds used there, and every other source has at least $\lceil L/e_j \rceil - 1 \ge L/e_j - 1$ heals strictly between $t_0$ and $t$ (at least $t/e_j - 1$ from the start, since $\varphi_j \le e_j$), so $\Delta > (r - \lambda)L - a - C$; part 2 uses $\sum_j c_j(\lfloor (t - \varphi_j)/e_j \rfloor + 1) < rt + C$. $\square$ *Label:* `DERIVED` for the model. *Test:* `test_t0305_corollary_several_heal_sources` (two sources, $a \in \{1, 2\}$, $c_1, c_2 \in \{1, 2\}$, $b, e_1, e_2 \le 4$, every pair of phases, $M \le 7$, both orders).

*Documented rates (HP per second, exact).* Damage: outside the zone, unsafe circle ([M-0536](../rules/damage.md#m-0536)), airstrike fire ([M-0413](../rules/viewer.md#m-0413)), robot ([M-0523](../rules/weapons.md#m-0523)): $1/2$; outside with a Hazmat suit ([M-0209](../rules/items.md#m-0209)): $1/4$; tear gas ([M-0410](../rules/viewer.md#m-0410)): $1/5$; guard zombie ([M-0449](../rules/zombies.md#m-0449)): $2/5$, at most 30 HP per contestant; zombie ([M-0441](../rules/zombies.md#m-0441)): $1/10$ each, at most 30 HP per zombie and contestant (`wiki-zombies-003`, [M-0635](../rules/zombies.md#m-0635)); poison ([M-0170](../rules/poison.md#m-0170)): $1/90$; bleeding ([M-0336](../rules/weapons.md#m-0336)): $1/300$; cyclops ([M-0426](../rules/viewer.md#m-0426)): 2. The two zombie drains are therefore finite adversaries (30 HP each; how the cap counts is [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008)), and the dichotomy applies to them only until the cap is reached. Renewable heals: regeneration $1/1800$ ([M-0652](../rules/healing.md#m-0652), [U-0007](../unknowns.md#u-0007) A), one healer's ability $1/600$ ([M-0651](../rules/healing.md#m-0651); not from inside a smoke cloud, [M-0227](../rules/items.md#m-0227), [U-0213](../unknowns.md#u-0213)). Finite heals: bandage $1/12$ for 2 min, Campfire 1 per person while the pool lasts ([T-0304](../theorems/T-0304.md)).

*Consequences* (`CONDITIONAL` on the readings named in each record).
- No renewable heal holds off any documented periodic damage: even regeneration plus one healer's ability ($1/450$) is below bleeding ($1/300$), the slowest damage. By the corollary, two healers on cooldown plus regeneration ($7/1800 \ge 6/1800$) cancel bleeding, which the ability cures anyway ([M-0651](../rules/healing.md#m-0651)); this also needs the healers to heal on a fixed 600 s clock, which is a modelling choice.
- A bandage ($1/12$) is faster than poison and bleeding, so while it lasts (2 min) the HP it pays exceeds what they take; it holds off nothing faster. After it ends, poison resumes; bleeding was cured by the bandage ([M-0234](../rules/healing.md#m-0234)).
- A Campfire (1 HP per second) is faster than every documented damage except the cyclops; how long it lasts against each is [T-0304](../theorems/T-0304.md), not [T-0305](../theorems/T-0305.md).
- Necromancy's drain transfer gives its owner up to $1/10$ HP per second per zombie and victim, 30 HP at most per pair ([M-0635](../rules/zombies.md#m-0635), [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008), [U-0613](../unknowns.md#u-0613)); chapter 07 treats it.

*Interaction with the hit pipeline.* If periodic ticks are hits ([U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002) A) and the defender is live on Kick, 1 HP ticks stay 1 HP under both readings of [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001) ([T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md), [T-0004](../theorems/T-0004.md)), so the rates above do not change for them. Two-HP ticks become 1 HP under [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001) A and stay 2 under B, halving tear gas and guard zombie rates under A; a cyclops hit of 10 becomes 9 under both. Body armor and a fortress absorb the first $f + a_{\text{armor}}$ HP of covered damage under [T-0007](../theorems/T-0007.md), which acts like a larger starting $h$ in part 2 and does not change any rate; this carries [T-0007](../theorems/T-0007.md)'s own conditions ([U-0101](../unknowns.md#u-0101) reading A, [M-0217](../rules/items.md#m-0217) reading A and [U-0102](../unknowns.md#u-0102), [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading A, [M-0529](../rules/damage.md#m-0529) reading A) and the open question of which damage kinds armor covers ([C-0102](../unknowns.md#c-0102)). These pipeline remarks are not proved or tested in this chapter; they restate [T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md), [T-0004](../theorems/T-0004.md) and [T-0007](../theorems/T-0007.md).

*Label:* `DERIVED` for the model; its use for Safe Zone is `CONDITIONAL` on the damage and heal sources being fixed-interval ticks with the stated phases, and on the readings of each rate's record. *Test:* `test_t0305_sustain_dichotomy` (exhaustive over $a, c \in \{1,2,3\}$, $b, e \in 1..6$, every phase, $M \le 9$, every $h \le M$, both orders; a repeated state (HP, time mod $\operatorname{lcm}(b, e)$) proves survival forever).
