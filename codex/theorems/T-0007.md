# T-0007

Extracted from [theory/01-hit-pipeline.md](../../theory/01-hit-pipeline.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0007](../theorems/T-0007.md).** Take a defender with a fortress of remaining durability $f$ and armor of remaining durability $a$, hit in order by $x_1, \dots, x_m \ge 1$ (values after step 5), with no repair or decay in between. Under reading A of [U-0101](../unknowns.md#u-0101) (excess of the hit that breaks a layer passes to the next layer):

- HP lost after the $j$-th hit: $\max(S_j - f - a,\ 0)$ with $S_j = x_1 + \dots + x_j$;
- fortress durability left: $f - \min(S_j, f)$; armor durability left: $a - \min(\max(S_j - f, 0), a)$.

*Proof.* By induction on $j$. Each layer absorbs $\min(x, \text{remaining})$ and passes the rest, so after any prefix the fortress has absorbed $\min(S_j, f)$ and the armor $\min(\max(S_j - f, 0), a)$: if the claim holds after $j-1$ hits, the $j$-th hit first fills what is left of the fortress, then what is left of the armor, then reaches HP, which is exactly the claim for $S_j = S_{j-1} + x_j$. $\square$

*Consequence.* Under reading A, the order and size of the hits do not matter: a fresh body armor (15, [M-0217](../rules/items.md#m-0217)) plus a full fortress (30, [M-0529](../rules/damage.md#m-0529)) absorb the first 45 HP of damage of any kind they cover, whether it comes as one 45 HP hit or as forty-five 1 HP ticks. Under reading B (the excess of the hit that breaks the armor is lost), a large hit on nearly broken armor is partly wasted, so big hits lose value against armor: the defender saves at most one hit's overflow over the whole sequence.

*Caveats.* Under [M-0529](../rules/damage.md#m-0529) reading A the fortress's 30 HP pool is shared with everyone within 20 m, so $f$ falls with other contestants' hits too; under reading B each protected contestant has their own 30 HP. Armor enchantment changes $a$ over time (+10 up to 45, then -1 every 20 min down to 15, merchant page), which this theorem excludes. Whether 1 HP ticks reach steps 6 and 7 at all is [U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002). A trapped gift ignores body armor (items page), against the damage page's "Body Armor works on every kind of damage" ([C-0102](../unknowns.md#c-0102)).

*Label:* `DERIVED` for the arithmetic. For the game it is `CONDITIONAL` on four readings at once: [U-0101](../unknowns.md#u-0101) reading A (the excess of a breaking hit passes on), [M-0217](../rules/items.md#m-0217) reading A and [U-0102](../unknowns.md#u-0102) (armor and fortress are depleting pools that lose 1 durability per HP absorbed), [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading A (every hit reaching step 6 is at least 1 HP), and [M-0529](../rules/damage.md#m-0529) reading A (one shared fortress pool). Under [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading B the formula fails: a single 1 HP hit with $f = 30$ still costs 1 HP. *Test:* `sim/tests/test_pipeline_theorems.py` (property test plus exhaustive check of all sequences of up to 3 hits of 1 to 12 HP).
