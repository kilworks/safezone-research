# T-0703

Extracted from [theory/07-zombies-and-pursuit.md](../../theory/07-zombies-and-pursuit.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0703](../theorems/T-0703.md).** Let $n$ regular zombies keep a contestant with $h$ HP in range from $t = 0$ on (for example a contestant standing still or slower than 1.4 m/s: [T-0701](../theorems/T-0701.md)(b)), all contacts starting at 0, no blockers and no body armor, and every zombie existing for the whole contact. Call the contestant killed when the drained total reaches $h$: this is the death-at-0-HP model of [T-0014](../theorems/T-0014.md) (0 HP absorbing); no KB record quotes the HP at which a contestant dies, so it is a modelling convention. A 1 HP tick is not changed by the 1 HP minimum ([M-0123](../rules/damage.md#m-0123)), nor by the Kick reduction under either reading of [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001) ([T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md)), so neither enters.

(a) Under [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) reading A (the 30 HP cap holds per zombie per contestant over the zombie's whole life), these zombies drain at most $30n$ HP from this contestant in total, so they can kill from $h$ HP only if $n \ge \lceil h/30 \rceil$. One zombie cannot kill anyone with 31 HP or more. At the base maximum HP of each sigil (90 for Greed, [M-0508](../rules/sigil.md#m-0508); 100 for most, [M-0602](../rules/sigil.md#m-0602) to [M-0606](../rules/sigil.md#m-0606) and [M-0515](../rules/sigil.md#m-0515), [M-0530](../rules/sigil.md#m-0530); 110 for Piety, [M-0500](../rules/sigil.md#m-0500)) it takes 3, 4 and 4 zombies; at 200 HP, the highest maximum HP the merchant sells to sigils other than Piety, it takes 7, and at 220 HP, the Piety ceiling, it takes 8 ([M-0333](../rules/economy.md#m-0333): "Maximum HP goes up to 200, or 220 for Piety"). Guard zombies have the same cap of 30 ([M-0449](../rules/zombies.md#m-0449)) and count the same way under [U-0615](../unknowns.md#u-0615) reading A; under [U-0615](../unknowns.md#u-0615) reading B all guard zombies together count once.

(b) If $h \le 30n$, let $k = \lceil h/n \rceil$. Death comes at $10k$ s under B1, at $10(k - 1)$ s under B0, and at a time in $[10(k-1), 10k]$ under reading A, whatever the phases. A horde of 50 in range kills from 100 HP in 10 to 20 s and from 220 HP in 40 to 50 s; four zombies kill from 100 HP in 240 to 250 s.

(c) A regular zombie needs 300 s of contact (B1) or 290 s (B0) to drain its full 30 HP, a guard zombie 75 s or 70 s. Count a zombie as existing up to and including the instant it expires (the convention of [T-0701](../theorems/T-0701.md)(d)). A contact that starts $\Lambda$ seconds before the zombie expires holds $N(\Lambda, \varphi)$ ticks ([T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md), closed range), so the lifetime cuts the drain whenever $\Lambda$ is below those times. A horde zombie (20 min, [M-0408](../rules/viewer.md#m-0408)) gives its full cap only to a contact that starts at most 900 s (B1) or 910 s (B0) after its release, if the 20 min count from the release ([U-0403](../unknowns.md#u-0403)); a contact starting 1000 s after the release drains 20 HP (B1) or 21 HP (B0). A guard zombie (1 h, [M-0633](../rules/zombies.md#m-0633)) gives its full cap to a contact starting at most 3525 s (B1) or 3530 s (B0) after it appears. Summoned, death and horde-bonus zombies have an unknown lifetime ([U-0612](../unknowns.md#u-0612), swept from 60 s to 86400 s), and the same subtraction applies.

(d) Under [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) reading B (the cap holds per encounter), the bound of (a) holds within one continuous contact only, since each new contact with the same zombie brings a fresh cap. Under reading C (the cap holds per time window of unknown length $W$), at most $30n$ HP per window. Under both, only the drain rate and the lifetime remain as bounds: under timing readings A and B1 one zombie drains at most $\lfloor \Lambda/10 \rfloor + 1$ ticks from one contestant over $\Lambda$ seconds of life, so the lifetime is the only bound on the total. Under B0 a contestant who leaves and re-enters range restarts the timer with an immediate drain at each entry ([T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md)(e)), and not even this rate bound is proven.

*Proof.* (a) Sum the caps. (b) By [T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md)(a), in $[0, t]$ each zombie has drained $\lfloor t/10 \rfloor$ or $\lfloor t/10 \rfloor + 1$ times, whatever its phase. At $t = 10k$ each has at least $k$ ticks, so the total is at least $nk \ge h$. At any $t < 10(k - 1)$ each has at most $k - 1$ ticks and the total is at most $n(k - 1) < h$, because $k - 1 < h/n$. With every $\varphi = P$ (B1) the $k$-th tick of every zombie is at $10k$, and with every $\varphi = 0$ (B0) it is at $10(k - 1)$. No zombie needs more than $k$ ticks, and $k \le 30$ because $h \le 30n$ means $h/n \le 30$. (c) [T-0010](../theorems/T-0010.md) for B1; under B0 the 30th tick of 1 HP is at 290 s and the 15th tick of 2 HP at 70 s. A contact of length $\Lambda$ holds $N(\Lambda, \varphi)$ ticks by [T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md); subtract the needed contact time from 1200 s and 3600 s. (d) Reading of the records for the caps. For the rate: under A the ticks of one zombie sit on one grid of period 10 s, so at most $\lfloor \Lambda/10 \rfloor + 1$ fall in $[0, \Lambda]$; under B1 separate contacts of lengths $\ell_i$ give $\sum_i \lfloor \ell_i/10 \rfloor \le \lfloor \Lambda/10 \rfloor$ ticks, as in [T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md)(e). $\square$

*Consequence.* Under the lifetime reading of the cap, zombies alone can kill only in groups: a lone zombie is a 30 HP tax, not a death sentence, while a horde of 50 caps at 1500 HP per contestant. If all 50 are within 5 m of a contestant at once from $t = 0$ (with no blocker, no armor and the death model above), they kill from any $h \le 250$ HP within 50 s under B1, by (b) with $k \le 5$; every maximum HP the merchant allows is at most 220 ([M-0333](../rules/economy.md#m-0333)), and whether current HP can exceed maximum HP is [U-0203](../unknowns.md#u-0203). In play, zombies chase the nearest contestant ([M-0440](../rules/zombies.md#m-0440)), so 50 at once on one contestant is the extreme case, not the typical one. The reading of [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) is decisive for slow contestants, since under reading B a single long-lived zombie that a contestant keeps re-meeting has no cap at all, only its lifetime. (This consequence is `CONDITIONAL` on the same records as the theorem.)

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) (reading A for (a) to (c)), [U-0425](../unknowns.md#u-0425) (the three timing cases), [U-0615](../unknowns.md#u-0615) for guards, [U-0403](../unknowns.md#u-0403) and [U-0612](../unknowns.md#u-0612) for (c), [M-0333](../rules/economy.md#m-0333) for the 200 and 220 HP ceilings, and the death-at-0-HP model (a modelling convention, as in [T-0014](../theorems/T-0014.md)). *Test:* `test_t0703_many_zombies` (tick-event simulation for every $h$ from 1 to 220 and every $n$ from 1 to 60, under B0 and B1, and random phases of A for every eleventh $h$, with the cap; for (c), contacts cut by the lifetime for every start second, against the latest starts that still reach the cap; for (d), the rate bound under A and B1 over random contact patterns).
