# T-0702

Extracted from [theory/07-zombies-and-pursuit.md](../../theory/07-zombies-and-pursuit.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md).** When the contestant flees in a straight line and the zombie pursues (the pair of strategies of [T-0701](../theorems/T-0701.md)), the number of drain ticks that land is

$$
N(L, \varphi) = \begin{cases} \lfloor (L - \varphi)/P \rfloor + 1 & \text{if } \varphi \le L \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \quad \text{(closed range)}, \qquad N = \begin{cases} \lceil (L - \varphi)/P \rceil & \text{if } \varphi < L \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \quad \text{(open range)},
$$

and the drain is $D = \delta \cdot \min(N, c)$ HP, before blockers, armor and the Kick reduction.

(a) For every $\varphi \in [0, P]$ (closed range), $N \in \{\lfloor L/P \rfloor, \lfloor L/P \rfloor + 1\}$: reading B1 gives the lower value, B0 the upper one, and reading A either one.

(b) If the unknown phase of reading A is taken as uniform on $[0, P)$ (a modelling assumption, not a rule), then $\mathbb{E}[N] = L/P$ exactly and $\Pr(N = \lfloor L/P \rfloor + 1) = L/P - \lfloor L/P \rfloor$, for $L < cP$ (before the cap can bind).

(c) Regular zombie ($\rho = 5$, $P = 10$, $u = 1.4$), reading B1, closed range: the escape costs 0 HP if and only if $v > 1.4 + (5 - r_0)/10$ m/s, that is 1.9 m/s from contact ($r_0 = 0$), 1.65 m/s from 2.5 m, 1.5 m/s from 4 m. With the open range, "if and only if $v \ge$" the same value. Under B0 no speed avoids the first HP.

(d) Guard zombie ($\rho = 10$, $P = 5$, $\delta = 2$), reading B1, closed range: 0 HP if and only if $v > (10 - r_0)/5$ when the guard stands still ([U-0429](../unknowns.md#u-0429) A), 2 m/s from the guard's own position; and $v > 1.4 + (10 - r_0)/5$ when it chases ([U-0429](../unknowns.md#u-0429) B), 3.4 m/s from contact.

(e) *No path does better, no chase does worse.* Against a pure-pursuit zombie, no contestant path with speed at most $v$ takes fewer than $N$ ticks, under every reading. Against straight flight, no zombie motion forces more than $N$ ticks under readings A and B1. Under B0 a zombie that left and re-entered range would start a new timer with an immediate drain at each entry; pure pursuit never does that against straight flight (the distance only grows), so the value $N$ stands, but the guarantee against arbitrary zombie motion fails under B0.

*Proof.* A tick at $\varphi + jP$ ($j = 0, 1, \dots$) lands while the contestant is in range, that is at a time at most $L$ (closed range) or below $L$ (open range), because under straight flight against pursuit $d(t) = r_0 + (v - u)t$ (both move on one line). Counting the integers $j \ge 0$ with $\varphi + jP \le L$ gives $\lfloor (L - \varphi)/P \rfloor + 1$; with $<$ it gives $\lceil (L - \varphi)/P \rceil$. (a) For $\varphi \in [0, P]$, $(L - \varphi)/P$ lies in $[L/P - 1, L/P]$, so its floor is $\lfloor L/P \rfloor - 1$ or $\lfloor L/P \rfloor$; when $\varphi > L$ we have $L < P$ and $N = 0 = \lfloor L/P \rfloor$. (b) $\int_0^P N(\varphi)\,d\varphi = \sum_{j \ge 0} \left|\{\varphi \in [0, P) : \varphi \le L - jP\}\right| = \sum_{j \ge 0} \min(\max(L - jP, 0), P) = L$; divide by $P$. The probability follows because $N$ takes only the two values of (a). (c) and (d): under B1 and the closed range, $N = 0$ if and only if $L < P$; solve for $v$. (e) By [T-0701](../theorems/T-0701.md), against pursuit the contestant is in range on all of $[0, L]$ whatever path is taken, and extra contact only adds ticks. Against straight flight the contact lies inside $[0, L]$; under A the ticks sit on a fixed grid; under B1 each separate contact of length $\ell_i$ gives $\lfloor \ell_i / P \rfloor$ ticks and $\sum_i \lfloor \ell_i/P \rfloor \le \lfloor L/P \rfloor$. $\square$

*Consequences.* (1) For a single regular zombie the stakes are small: under B1 a contestant at 1.9 m/s or faster from contact loses nothing, and under every reading the loss is $L/10$ HP on average, plus or minus one tick. The cap of 30 HP binds only if the escape takes 300 s, that is $v \le 1.4 + 5/300 \approx 1.4167$ m/s from contact. (2) Starting distance matters as much as speed: 2.5 m of head start halves $L$. (3) For guard zombies, whether they chase ([U-0429](../unknowns.md#u-0429)) decides everything: at 1.5 m/s a still guard costs 2 HP (B1), a chasing one its full 30 HP cap.

*What $D$ becomes in HP.* $D$ is the drain before protections. A Shield, a Safety bubble, a Reality Shield, sleeping in one's own Sleeping Bag, the Necromancy sigil or a Zombie repellent make it 0 ([M-0145](../rules/damage.md#m-0145), [M-0213](../rules/items.md#m-0213), [M-0435](../rules/viewer.md#m-0435), [M-0454](../rules/zombies.md#m-0454), [M-0636](../rules/zombies.md#m-0636); for the repellent see [C-0205](../unknowns.md#c-0205) and [C-0402](../unknowns.md#c-0402)); body armor absorbs it ([M-0145](../rules/damage.md#m-0145)), by [T-0007](../theorems/T-0007.md) if drains are hits ([U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002)). For a contestant live on Kick, a 1 HP tick is unchanged under both readings of [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001) ([T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md)). A 2 HP guard tick becomes 1 HP under [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001) reading A ($\lfloor 9 \cdot 2/10 \rfloor = 1$, [T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md)) if drains go through the pipeline ([U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002) reading A), and stays 2 HP under reading B. Whether the 30 HP cap counts the nominal drain or the HP actually lost after armor and the Kick reduction is open question Q7-4.
