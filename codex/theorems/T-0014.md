# T-0014

Extracted from [theory/05-survival-processes.md](../../theory/05-survival-processes.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0014](../theorems/T-0014.md).** Suppose that from every state $h \in \{1, \dots, M\}$, one step loses at least $c \ge 1$ HP with probability at least $q > 0$. Then from every state the chain reaches 0 with probability 1, and the expected time to reach it is at most $m / q^m$ steps, where $m = \lceil M / c \rceil$.

*Proof.* From any state, $m$ consecutive losses of at least $c$ reach 0; this has probability at least $q^m$. Split time into blocks of $m$ steps: each block kills with probability at least $q^m$ whatever happened before, so survival after $k$ blocks is at most $(1 - q^m)^k \to 0$, and the expected number of blocks is at most $1/q^m$. $\square$

This is the discrete form of a classical fact from risk theory: a surplus process with a reflecting upper barrier (here, max HP) is ruined with probability 1 (the dividend-barrier models of ruin theory; reference not verified here). The proof above does not depend on that literature.

*Consequence: "will I eventually die" is the wrong question.* The bound can be astronomically large. `sim/tests/test_markov.py` computes the exact expected time to death $t = N\mathbf{1}$, $N = (I - Q)^{-1}$, for example chains: with $M = 100$, a 1 HP loss with probability 0.05 per step and a 5 HP heal with probability 0.5, the expected time from full HP is astronomically large (the $2 \times 10^{16}$ first printed here was a floating-point artifact; exact computation pending). Death is certain and irrelevant. The quantities that matter for the Codex are the finite-horizon survival $\Pr(\tau > T)$ over the event's duration $T$ (unknown: [U-0009](../unknowns.md#u-0009)) and the expected time to death $\mathbb{E}[\tau]$, both computed by the simulator for each scenario.

*Label:* `DERIVED` (a mathematical fact about the model). Its use for Safe Zone is `CONDITIONAL` on the environment having a loss probability bounded below, which holds whenever any damage source can reach the contestant. *Test:* `test_t0014_capped_hp_means_certain_death`.
