**Theorem [T-0402](../theorems/T-0402.md).** Assume the loss $\ell$ runs through the hit pipeline ([M-0117](../rules/damage.md#m-0117)): Kick reduction at step 4 (if live), then Body armor of remaining durability $\alpha$ at step 7, which absorbs $\min(L, \alpha)$ of the loss $L$ that reaches it ([U-0101](../unknowns.md#u-0101) reading A). Write $L = \ell$ if not live, $L = \lfloor 9\ell/10 \rfloor$ ([U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001) reading A) or $L = \ell - \lfloor \ell/10 \rfloor$ (reading B) if live.

(a) With an active Shield, Sleeping Bag immunity, or while standing inside a Safety bubble (the bubble protects "Everyone inside", [M-0213](../rules/items.md#m-0213)), $\Delta h = +a$ with probability $1/10$ and $0$ with probability $9/10$: $\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] = a/10 \ge 0$, and the drink never kills. A vulnerable contestant gets no protection from a Shield or Safety bubble ([M-0129](../rules/damage.md#m-0129), [M-0326](../rules/items.md#m-0326)). When several blockers apply, which one is used up is [U-0120](../unknowns.md#u-0120); it does not change HP.

(b) With armor of remaining durability $\alpha$ read as a depleting pool ([U-0209](../unknowns.md#u-0209) reading A), the HP lost on a failure is $\min(\max(L - \alpha, 0), h)$ and the armor loses $\min(L, \alpha)$ durability, provided the minimum of 1 HP is applied at step 5, before armor ([U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading A; [M-0123](../rules/damage.md#m-0123) reading A). Fresh armor ($\alpha = 15$, [M-0217](../rules/items.md#m-0217)) at $M = 100$ turns the loss of 30 into 15 ($\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] = -17/2$ at $h = 50$). Armor enchanted to durability 45 ([M-0339](../rules/items.md#m-0339)) absorbs the whole loss for every reachable $M \le 150$ ($\ell \le 45$): HP never drops, and the expected durability spent per drink is $9\ell/10$ (27 at $M = 100$). Three conditions on that sentence: 45 is reachable only if the enchantment can be bought three times ([U-0338](../unknowns.md#u-0338) readings A or C; under reading B one purchase gives at most 25, which absorbs no reachable $\ell$ fully since $\ell \ge 27$); 45 is a peak, since enchanted armor loses one durability every twenty minutes down to 15 ([M-0339](../rules/items.md#m-0339); clock details [U-0339](../unknowns.md#u-0339)); and under [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading B (the 1 HP minimum applies to the final loss, [M-0123](../rules/damage.md#m-0123) reading B) a fully absorbed failure still costs exactly 1 HP, so HP drops by 1 on each failure ($\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] = 41/10$ at $M = 100$, $h = 50$, $\alpha \ge 30$). Under [U-0209](../unknowns.md#u-0209) reading B (a per-hit cap of 15, never depleted) each failure loses $\max(L - 15, 0)$ HP, or at least 1 HP under [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading B, and no durability is spent.

(c) The two Kick readings give the same $L$ iff $10 \mid \ell$ ([T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md)); among reachable $M$, iff $M \in \{100, 200\}$. Otherwise the Kick reduction of reading B removes one HP less than that of reading A, so the loss $L$ that remains is one HP larger under reading B:

| $M$ | 90 | 100 | 110 | 120 | 130 | 140 | 150 | 160 | 170 | 180 | 190 | 200 | 210 | 220 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $L$, reading A | 24 | 27 | 29 | 32 | 35 | 37 | 40 | 43 | 45 | 48 | 51 | 54 | 56 | 59 |
| $L$, reading B | 25 | 27 | 30 | 33 | 36 | 38 | 41 | 44 | 46 | 49 | 52 | 54 | 57 | 60 |

(d) A Protection contestant, if "Potions heal you 50% less" covers Eddie's potion ([U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) reading B or C; not under reading A), heals $g_P = \lfloor g/2 \rfloor$, $\lceil g/2 \rceil$ or exactly $g/2$ ([M-0645](../rules/healing.md#m-0645) readings A, B, C; [U-0618](../unknowns.md#u-0618)). For reachable $M$ the three agree, $g_P = M/4$, iff $20 \mid M$; for $M \in \{90, 110, \dots, 210\}$ they differ, and reading C leaves a non-integer HP after a success ($g_P = 55/2$ at $M = 110$). [T-0401](../theorems/T-0401.md)(a) and the positive-mean part of [T-0401](../theorems/T-0401.md)(b) hold with $g_P$ in place of $g$ and $h_E = \lceil g_P/9 \rceil - 1$ (the largest $h$ with $9h < g_P$; for integer $g_P$ this is $\lfloor (g_P - 1)/9 \rfloor$): $h_E = 2$ at $M = 100$; at $M = 110$, $h_E = 2$ under floor and $3$ under ceiling or exact.

*Proof.* (a) A blocker sets the hit to 0 at step 1 ([M-0118](../rules/damage.md#m-0118)), so the failure branch leaves $h$ unchanged. (b) Step 4 then step 7 with [U-0101](../unknowns.md#u-0101) reading A and [U-0209](../unknowns.md#u-0209) reading A, as in [T-0007](../theorems/T-0007.md); under [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading A step 5 only raises a loss of 0 to 1 before armor, and $L \ge 24$ for reachable $M$, so it changes nothing; under [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103) reading B the final loss is $\max(1, \cdot)$. (c) [T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md): $R_B(d) - R_A(d) = \mathbb{1}[10 \nmid d]$; the table is computed. (d) The argument of [T-0401](../theorems/T-0401.md)(b) for $h < \ell$ uses only $9h < g$ versus $9h \ge g$ and $g < M/2 + 1$, which hold with $g_P \le g$; the test checks every reachable $M$ under the three readings. $\square$

*Consequence.* Under this reading, drinking while shielded is a free 10% lottery on a heal of half your max HP, and an enchanted armor makes it nearly free. Whether the game agrees is exactly [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004); one VOD of a shielded contestant drinking decides (a).
