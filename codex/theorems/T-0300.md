# T-0300

Extracted from [theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md](../../theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0300](../theorems/T-0300.md).** For every reachable max HP $M \in \{90, 100, \dots, 220\}$ (for Protection, $M \in \{100, \dots, 200\}$), the rounding readings of a heal give different HP in exactly these cases:

1. *Protection, small potion (every reading of [U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617)).* Floor, ceiling and the exact half ([U-0618](../unknowns.md#u-0618) readings A, B, C) give 2, 3 or 2.5 HP. On the medium, large and gigantic tiers they agree (5, 10, 20 HP).
2. *Protection, Eddie's potion ([U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) B or C).* The 10% branch heals $M/2$, and the cut halves it to $M/4$. The three readings differ if and only if $M \equiv 10 \pmod{20}$, by floor and ceiling 1 HP apart with the exact value half-way: at $M = 110$, 27, 28 or 27.5 HP.
3. *Protection, healing circle ([U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) C).* Half of 25 HP is 12, 13 or 12.5 HP. Half of God's Fruit (20 HP) and of a bandage's 10 HP total is a whole number. Whether the cut is applied to a bandage's total or to each 1 HP or 2 HP tick (where the floor reading gives 0 or 1 HP per tick) is not decided by any record; [T-0303](../theorems/T-0303.md) assumes the bandage is not cut ([U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) A or B).
4. *Death's gravestone rob.* $\lfloor M/20 \rfloor$, $\lceil M/20 \rceil$ and the exact $M/20$ ([U-0600](../unknowns.md#u-0600) readings A, B, D) differ if and only if $M \equiv 10 \pmod{20}$: floor and ceiling by exactly 1 HP, the exact value half-way. Round half up (reading C) equals the ceiling. For a Death contestant ($M$ from 100 to 200) the floor reading gives 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 9, 9, 10 HP.
5. *No other case.* Without the Protection cut, $M/2$ (Eddie's heal), $3M/10$ (Eddie's loss) and $6M/10$ (Death's 60% cap) are integers, so every rounding reading of [U-0204](../unknowns.md#u-0204) and of [U-0603](../unknowns.md#u-0603) gives one value. The Piety bonus has a single reading by [T-0005](../theorems/T-0005.md) and is not restated here.

*Hypotheses.* Max HP only changes through the base values and +10 upgrades. The list of heals is the table of section 3.0.

*Proof.* Write $M = 10j$. (1) $x/2$ is an integer exactly when $x$ is even; among 5, 10, 20, 40 only 5 is odd. (2) $M/4 = 5j/2$ is an integer iff $j$ is even, i.e. $20 \mid M$; otherwise it is a half-integer. (3) 25 is odd; 20 and 10 are even. (4) $5M/100 = j/2$, the same parity argument; round half up of a half-integer goes up. (5) $M/2 = 5j$, $3M/10 = 3j$, $6M/10 = 6j$. $\square$

*Consequence.* Off the grid the readings split (for example $3 \cdot 95/10 = 28.5$), so any future max-HP modifier that is not a multiple of 10 reopens [U-0204](../unknowns.md#u-0204) and [U-0603](../unknowns.md#u-0603).

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on the reachable-$M$ assumption ([M-0333](../rules/economy.md#m-0333), [U-0330](../unknowns.md#u-0330)); items 2 and 3 name the reading of [U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) they need. *Test:* `test_t0300_where_heal_rounding_matters` (every reachable $M$, every heal of the list, every reading of [U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617), [U-0618](../unknowns.md#u-0618) and [U-0600](../unknowns.md#u-0600); the set of cases that split is asserted exactly).
