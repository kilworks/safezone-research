# T-0013

Extracted from [theory/05-survival-processes.md](../../theory/05-survival-processes.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0013](../theorems/T-0013.md).** A contestant moving straight away at $v > 1.4$ m/s from distance $r_0 \le 5$ m leaves drain range after $(5 - r_0)/(v - 1.4)$ s, in a straight-line pursuit model. At a brisk 2 m/s from contact, that is 8.3 s, before the first 10 s tick (under the reading that the first tick comes one interval after contact). Below 1.4 m/s a zombie that keeps chasing catches up. It chases the nearest valid contestant "at any distance" ([U-0424](../unknowns.md#u-0424)) until its time runs out ([M-0450](../rules/zombies.md#m-0450), [U-0403](../unknowns.md#u-0403)), so a stationary contestant is reached unless the zombie expires or picks someone nearer first.

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0425](../unknowns.md#u-0425) (tick timing), [U-0424](../unknowns.md#u-0424) and [U-0403](../unknowns.md#u-0403); the pursuit model is a simplification (straight line, no obstacles), not a rule. *Test:* `test_t0013_zombie_escape` checks the arithmetic only.
