# T-0303

Extracted from [theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md](../../theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0303](../theorems/T-0303.md).** With each tick capped at max HP and no damage, a bandage restores $m$ missing HP after the time in the table, and wastes its total minus $m$ (total 10 HP unless stated). Write $f_X(m)$ for the time under [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) reading $X$:

| Reading | Time to full (s) | Total (HP) |
|---|---|---|
| [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) A | $f_A(m) = 12m$ | 10 |
| [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) B | $f_B(m) = 12(m - 1)$ | 10 |
| [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) C | $f_C(m) = 24 \lceil m/2 \rceil$ | 10 |
| [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) D | $f_D(m) = 12m$ | 10 |
| Protection, [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) A, with [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) $X$ | $\tfrac{11}{10} f_X(m)$: $13.2m$, $13.2(m - 1)$, $26.4 \lceil m/2 \rceil$, $13.2m$ | 10 |
| Protection, [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) B, with [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) $X$ | $f_X(m)$ | 11 for A, B, D; 10 for C |
| Protection, [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) C, with [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) $X$ | $f_X(m)$ | 11 for A, B, D; 10 or 12 for C |

A deficit above the total is never filled by one bandage. Across the readings, the time to fill a given $m \le 10$ spreads by 12 s for even $m$ and 24 s for odd $m$ ([U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) B against C); for a Protection contestant, across every pair of a [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) and a [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) reading, it spreads by $(6m + 60)/5$ s for even $m$ and $(6m + 126)/5$ s for odd $m$, at most 36 s (at $m = 9$: 96 s under [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) B or C with [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) B, 132 s under [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) A with [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) C).

*Proof.* The $k$-th HP arrives at the $k$-th tick time of each schedule; reading C delivers HP in pairs, so the $m$-th HP arrives with tick $\lceil m/2 \rceil$; reading D reaches $m$ HP at $m$ divided by the rate. [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) A multiplies every tick time by 1.1. Under [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) B and C the ticks keep their times, and only the number of ticks changes: $132/12 = 11$ ticks on a 12 s grid; on the 24 s grid of [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) C, $132/24 = 5.5$, so 5 whole ticks (B, and C rounded down) or 6 (C rounded up). Under [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214) D there is no grid and C is B. With no damage, HP paid after HP is full is wasted, which leaves the total minus $m$. The spreads are the differences of the largest and smallest rows, computed for every $m$. $\square$

*Consequence.* The schedules differ on the time to full by up to 24 s (36 s for Protection), which only matters when the bandage races a damage tick. While a bandage lasts (2 min, Protection 132 s) it gives $1/12$ HP per second (Protection [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) A: $5/66$); by [T-0305](../theorems/T-0305.md) that is enough to hold off poison ($1/90$) and bleeding ($1/300$) for that time, but not one zombie ($1/10$). Bleeding stops with the bandage ([M-0234](../rules/healing.md#m-0234)); poison does not, and resumes when the bandage ends. If Protection's 50% cut covers bandages ([U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) reading C), every Protection row above changes again ([T-0300](../theorems/T-0300.md), item 3).

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0214](../unknowns.md#u-0214), [U-0619](../unknowns.md#u-0619) (every reading in the table), [U-0617](../unknowns.md#u-0617) A or B, and [U-0203](../unknowns.md#u-0203) reading A. *Test:* `test_t0303_bandage_time_to_full` (every reading pair, every $m$ up to the total, time and waste; reading D is built as a separate 0.2 s grid, not from the A ticks; the two spreads are asserted for every $m$).
