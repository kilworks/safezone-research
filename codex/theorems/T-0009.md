**Theorem [T-0009](../theorems/T-0009.md).** (a) The dart's numbers agree with the poison page: 20 ticks at 90 s intervals end exactly at 1800 s = 30 min. (b) If the first tick comes 90 s after each (re)poisoning ([U-0115](../unknowns.md#u-0115) reading A; both readings of [M-0171](../rules/poison.md#m-0171) restart the 90 s countdown), a contestant who is re-poisoned at least once every 89 s never takes any poison damage.

*Proof.* (a) Ticks at $90, 180, \dots, 1800$ s: 20 ticks, the last at 1800 s. (b) The next tick is due 90 s after the last restart; a restart every 89 s or less comes first every time. $\square$
*Consequence.* Under reading "reset the tick clock", chaining Poison Darts or fake gravestones on the same target is worse than useless: each new poisoning delays the damage the previous one would have done. Under [U-0115](../unknowns.md#u-0115) reading B (first tick at once) each re-poisoning ticks immediately, and under reading C (a global 90 s grid) re-poisoning does not move the ticks: in both cases chaining keeps the damage flowing. One observation of a re-poisoned contestant decides it.
*Label:* (a) and (b) `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0115](../unknowns.md#u-0115) reading A (under reading B the 20 ticks end at 1710 s, not 1800 s). *Test:* `test_t0009_poison_clock`.
