# T-0707

Extracted from [theory/07-zombies-and-pursuit.md](../../theory/07-zombies-and-pursuit.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0707](../theorems/T-0707.md).** (a) Against a contestant standing still, the pursuit path is the straight segment from $Z_0$ to $E$.

(b) *Entry lemma.* Let $x > 10$. If the segment comes within 10 m of $M$, the zombie is at least $\sqrt{x^2 - 100}$ m from $E$ when it sets the mine off, and this is sharp: a walk that just grazes the mine's disk triggers it at exactly that distance.

(c) If $x > 5\sqrt{5} \approx 11.18$ m and the segment comes within 10 m of $M$, the mine goes off and kills the zombie before it is ever within 5 m of $E$, and $E$, more than 10 m from the mine, takes no blast damage. The contestant loses 0 HP under every timing reading. The threshold $5\sqrt{5} = \sqrt{10^2 + 5^2}$ is sharp: for $10 < x < 5\sqrt{5}$ a grazing walk is within 5 m of $E$ before it triggers the mine, and at $x = 5\sqrt{5}$ a grazing walk triggers it at the very instant it is exactly 5 m from $E$ (in drain range for that one instant under the closed range of Q7-2, never under the open range).

(d) If $10 < x \le 5\sqrt{5}$ and the segment comes within 10 m of $M$, the zombie can be within 5 m of $E$ before the mine goes off for at most $(5 - \sqrt{x^2 - 100})/1.4$ s, which is under $25/7 \approx 3.57$ s (0 s at $x = 5\sqrt{5}$, 0.298 s at $x = 11$, 1.285 s at $x = 10.5$, computed): 0 HP under B1, at most one tick under A and B0. If the segment does not come within 10 m of $M$, the mine never goes off and this part says nothing: at $x = 11$, a zombie walking in from the side opposite the mine passes no closer than 11 m to it and reaches $E$.

(e) The segment does come within 10 m of $M$ whenever $|Z_0 - E| \ge x$ and the angle at $E$ between the directions to $Z_0$ and to $M$ is at most $\arcsin(10/x)$: a cone of half-angle 63.43 degrees at $x = 5\sqrt{5}$, 56.44 at $x = 12$, 41.81 at $x = 15$, 30 at $x = 20$ and 19.47 at $x = 30$ (computed).

(f) A Protection contestant can stand on its own mine ($x = 0$): the first zombie that walks in from outside 10 m sets it off at 10 m, before it is within 5 m, whatever path it takes, and the blast kills every zombie then within 10 m. The contestant loses 0 HP to those zombies under every timing reading and takes no blast damage ([M-0640](../rules/sigil.md#m-0640)). A zombie that arrives after the blast is covered only if the mine has a use left ([U-0304](../unknowns.md#u-0304)).

*Proof.* (a) With $E$ fixed, the direction $e$ from the zombie to $E$ does not change as the zombie moves along it, so the path is a straight segment, walked at constant speed with the distance to $E$ decreasing. (b) Let $P$ be the first point of the segment within 10 m of $M$; it lies on the circle $|P - M| = 10$, and since the zombie was outside just before, its direction of travel does not point away from the centre: with $a = E - P$ (the rest of the walk, along that direction) and $b = M - P$, we have $a \cdot b \ge 0$. Then $x^2 = |a - b|^2 = |a|^2 - 2a \cdot b + 100$, so $|P - E|^2 = |a|^2 = x^2 - 100 + 2a \cdot b \ge x^2 - 100$, with equality when $a \cdot b = 0$, a walk tangent to the circle. (c) By (b) the trigger happens at a distance $\sqrt{x^2 - 100} > 5$ from $E$ or more, and along the segment the distance to $E$ only decreases, so no earlier point is within 5 m of $E$. The blast covers points within 10 m of $M$, and $x > 10$. A tangent walk triggers at $\sqrt{x^2 - 100}$, which is below 5 when $x < 5\sqrt{5}$ and equal to 5 when $x = 5\sqrt{5}$. (d) The zombie is within 5 m of $E$ before the trigger only while its distance to $E$ falls from 5 to at least $\sqrt{x^2 - 100}$, at 1.4 m/s. A contact shorter than one period $P$ holds at most one tick, and none under B1. If the segment stays more than 10 m from $M$, nothing in the model sets the mine off, and the zombie walks on to $E$. (e) The point of the ray from $E$ toward $Z_0$ nearest to $M$ lies at distance $x\cos\alpha$ from $E$ and $x\sin\alpha \le 10$ from $M$, where $\alpha$ is the angle; it is on the segment because $x\cos\alpha \le x \le |Z_0 - E|$. (f) A path from more than 10 m to within 5 m of $M = E$ passes through distance exactly 10 m first (intermediate value theorem), whatever the path; the first such zombie triggers the mine there, and Protection is immune to the blast ([M-0640](../rules/sigil.md#m-0640)). $\square$

*Consequence.* Placed between a contestant and an approaching zombie, a landmine is a free kill: stand still more than 11.18 m behind it, with the zombie inside the cone of (e), and the zombie dies on the way in. The naive bound from the triangle inequality ($x > 10 + 5 = 15$ m) is not sharp, because a walk toward the contestant enters the mine's disk on its far side. A Protection contestant gets the same for every direction by standing on its own mine. The same "10 m before 5 m" argument as (f) is why the Ingenuity Zombie Killer, which kills zombies that come within 10 m ([M-0453](../rules/zombies.md#m-0453), [M-0527](../rules/zombies.md#m-0527)), stops a walking zombie before it can drain; zombies that appear inside 10 m (horde zombies at random points, [M-0445](../rules/zombies.md#m-0445); death zombies within 10 m of a Necromancer, [M-0447](../rules/zombies.md#m-0447)) never "come within" and are covered by neither argument: whether such a zombie sets off a landmine is open question Q7-5, and whether the Zombie Killer counts it is [U-0522](../unknowns.md#u-0522). One mine kills only the zombies inside its blast at that moment; under [U-0304](../unknowns.md#u-0304) reading A (single use) it is then used up.

*Label:* (b) and (e) `DERIVED` (plane geometry). (a), (c), (d) and the use of (b) and (e) for zombies `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0424](../unknowns.md#u-0424) (pure pursuit, the zombie keeping $E$ as its target), [U-0300](../unknowns.md#u-0300) for the mine's 10 m (closed; under the open reading a grazing walk never sets the mine off), Q7-2 for the 5 m drain boundary and, for (d), [U-0425](../unknowns.md#u-0425). (f) `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0300](../unknowns.md#u-0300), [M-0640](../rules/sigil.md#m-0640) (Protection immune to landmines), [U-0621](../unknowns.md#u-0621) and, for zombies arriving after the first blast, [U-0304](../unknowns.md#u-0304); it needs no pursuit model. *Test:* `test_t0707_landmine` (entry points of straight walks from a lattice of starts and from grazing starts, solved as a quadratic, for nine mine distances including $x = 5\sqrt{5}$ and three directions; walks that miss the mine disk checked against their closest approach, with the $x = 11$ example as a control; the cone condition in exact rational arithmetic; random zombie paths for (f)).
