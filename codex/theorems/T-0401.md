# T-0401

Extracted from [theory/04-probabilistic-items.md](../../theory/04-probabilistic-items.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0401](../theorems/T-0401.md).** Let $a = \min(g, M - h)$ (HP actually gained on a success) and $b = \min(\ell, h)$ (HP actually lost on a failure).

(a) The HP change $\Delta h$ takes the value $+a$ with probability $1/10$ and $-b$ with probability $9/10$, so
$$\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] = \frac{a - 9b}{10}, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(\Delta h) = \frac{9\,(a + b)^2}{100},$$
and the drink kills with probability $9/10$ if $h \le \ell$, never if $h > \ell$.

(b) For every $M$ from 4 to 220 and every rounding pair of [U-0204](../unknowns.md#u-0204): $\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] > 0$ if and only if $h \le h_E := \lfloor (g - 1)/9 \rfloor$, and every such $h$ is at most $\ell$, so whenever the expected HP change is positive, a drink kills with probability $9/10$. For $h \ge \ell$, $\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] \le (g - 9\ell)/10 < 0$, with equality for $\ell \le h \le M - g$; for reachable $M$ this maximum is $-11M/50$ ($-22$ HP at $M = 100$).

(c) Under [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004) reading C (the loss cannot take HP below 1), $b = \min(\ell, h - 1)$, the drink never kills, and $\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] > 0$ iff $h \le h_E + 1$.

For reachable $M$, $h_E = 4, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 9, 9, 10, 11, 11, 12$ for $M = 90, 100, \dots, 220$. At $M = 100$: from full HP $\mathbb{E}[\Delta h] = -27$ with variance 81 (standard deviation 9); from $h = 50$, $-22$; from $h = 1$, $+41/10$ HP while dying with probability $9/10$.

*Proof.* (a) Two-point law: mean $\frac{1}{10}a - \frac{9}{10}b$; variance $p(1-p)(a+b)^2$ with $p = 1/10$. Death iff $h - \ell \le 0$. (b) If $h \ge \ell$ then $b = \ell$ and $a \le g$, so $\mathbb{E} \le (g - 9\ell)/10$, with equality iff $a = g$, iff $h \le M - g$ (the range $\ell \le h \le M - g$ is nonempty for reachable $M$, since $3M/10 \le M/2$; the test checks that the maximum is attained). $g < 9\ell$ for $M \ge 4$ (the test checks every case; by hand $g \le M/2 + 1 < 27M/10 - 9 \le 9\ell$ once $M \ge 5$). If $h < \ell$ then $b = h$ and $\mathbb{E} > 0$ iff $a > 9h$. If $9h \ge g$, then $a \le g \le 9h$: not positive. If $9h < g$, then $h \le (g-1)/9 < M/18$, so $M - h > 9h$ and $a = \min(g, M - h) > 9h$: positive. Each such $h$ is below $M/18 < \ell$ (checked for every $M \ge 4$ by the test). (c) Same argument with $b = \min(\ell, h - 1)$: positive iff $9(h - 1) < g$. $\square$

*Consequence.* "Positive expected HP" is the wrong criterion. The average is positive only because a dead contestant cannot lose more than the HP they had: at 1 HP you risk 1 HP to win 50. A contestant who drinks at low HP is not making a good bet on HP; they are trading a 90% chance of death now for a 10% chance of a large heal, and only survival probabilities can say whether that trade is good ([T-0403](../theorems/T-0403.md)).

*Label:* (a) and (b) `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004) reading A, [U-0203](../unknowns.md#u-0203) reading A and the roll being a true 1/10 draw (Q4.1). (c) `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004) reading C, [U-0203](../unknowns.md#u-0203) reading A and Q4.1. Parts (a), (b) and (c) each hold under every reading of [U-0204](../unknowns.md#u-0204) (all nine rounding pairs are tested); the list of $h_E$ values for reachable $M$ also uses [T-0400](../theorems/T-0400.md)(b). *Test:* `test_t0401_exact_law_of_one_drink` (every $M$ from 4 to 220, every $h \le M$, nine rounding pairs, both readings of [U-0004](../unknowns.md#u-0004)).
