# T-0705

**Theorem [T-0705](../theorems/T-0705.md).** Put the gravestone at the origin and the guard at $G$ with $|G| = 10$. A point at distance $s \ge 0$ from the gravestone, in a direction at angle $\beta$ from the direction of the guard, is within 10 m of the guard if and only if $s \le 20 \cos\beta$. The guard's drain disk is the disk of diameter 20 m that has the gravestone on its edge. Hence:

(a) The gravestone itself is exactly 10 m from the guard: drained under the closed range, not under the open range.

(b) No point on the far side of the gravestone (the closed half-plane facing away from the guard, $\cos\beta \le 0$), other than the gravestone itself, is in range. A looter who approaches from the side opposite the guard and stays strictly on that side is never drained; reaching the gravestone itself brings them back to the boundary case of (a).

(c) If the guard's direction is uniformly distributed (an assumption: the wiki gives no direction), a contestant standing at a fixed distance $s \in (0, 20]$ from the gravestone when the guard appears is in its range with probability $\arccos(s/20)/\pi$: 0.4841 at 1 m, 0.4681 at 2 m, 0.4196 at 5 m, $1/3$ at 10 m, and 0 beyond 20 m. As $s \to 0$ it tends to $1/2$.

*Proof.* $|P - G|^2 = s^2 - 20 s \cos\beta + 100$, which is at most 100 if and only if $s^2 \le 20 s\cos\beta$, that is $s = 0$ or $s \le 20\cos\beta$. (a) $s = 0$ gives equality. (b) If $s > 0$ and $\cos\beta \le 0$, then $s^2 > 0 \ge 20 s\cos\beta$. (c) With $\beta$ uniform on $[0, 2\pi)$ the condition $\cos\beta \ge s/20$ holds on an arc of length $2\arccos(s/20)$. $\square$

*Consequence.* Whether a looter at the grave is drained turns on a boundary convention, and the side of approach matters more than the distance. With a still guard, leaving the range from the guard's own position costs 0 HP under B1 above 2 m/s ([T-0702](../theorems/T-0702.md)(d)); from the gravestone ($r_0 = 10$ only if the guard appears at exactly 10 m, Q7-3; then $L = 0$) it costs 0 HP under B1, 0 HP under A unless the phase is exactly 0, and one tick of 2 HP under B0 with the closed range. If guards chase ([U-0429](../unknowns.md#u-0429) reading B), this theorem describes only the moment the guard appears.

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0429](../unknowns.md#u-0429) reading A, Q7-3 (the exact 10 m spawn distance) and Q7-2 (the closed or open range). Part (c) adds the uniform direction, a `HYPOTHESIS`. *Test:* `test_t0705_guard_disk` (every point of a half-metre lattice over a 50 m square, compared with the direct distance; the probability against rotated guards).
