# RE-0002: image assets split across two event folders

Snapshot: 2026-11-11. Labels as marked.

## Question

Which asset URLs do the pages reference that the visible text does not mention?

## Method

All `<img src>` URLs on the 20 saved pages (`tools/fetch_corpus.py`, `referenced_assets`), fetched once and hashed in `corpus/manifest.json` (50 images on `files.iceposeidon.com`, 2,244,266 bytes). File names compared with the item, weapon, attack and sigil names of the wiki text.

## Result

- `DOCUMENTED`: 24 item icons live under `events/safe-zone/icons/` (anti poison, bandage, body armor, drone, Eddie's potion, EMP, everything potion, flak jacket, flashbang, guardian angel, hazmat, health potion, landmine, long sword, machete, mirror forcefield, poison dart, safety bubble, shield, smoke bomb, thermal goggles, Zarok's axe, zombie repellent, assassin's knife) plus one marketing card.
- `DOCUMENTED`: 22 icons live under `events/safezone-miami/icons/`: airstrike, arrow, black hole, C4, campfire, cyclops, enchantment, fortress, gas mask, gunship, hunter's bow, robot, wizard's staff, zombie killer, and the nine sigils; plus two marketing images (`miami-wordmark.webp`, `duncle-hill-massacre.webp`).
- Every icon matches an item documented in the current text. No icon for an undocumented item was found among page-referenced images.
- `HYPOTHESIS` H-0002: the `safe-zone` folder belongs to the first (Austin, October 2026) edition and the `safezone-miami` folder to the second, so the 24 icons of the first folder are reused from Austin. Wayback captures of October 2026 (`corpus/diffs/`) show an Austin wiki that already mentions a Trolling sigil, so "the sigils are new in Miami" does not follow from the folder split.
- Consequence for the project: none. The October rules are obsolete; only the 2026-11-11 snapshot is a rule source (decision of the project owner, 2026-11-11). U-0013 is closed as irrelevant to the rule model.

## Bounded negative

Images referenced by CSS (`background-image`) and images not on the 20 pages were not fetched (`SCOPE.md` allows `<img>` only).
