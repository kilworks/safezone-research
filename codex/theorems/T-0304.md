# T-0304

Extracted from [theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md](../../theory/03-healing-and-sustain.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0304](../theorems/T-0304.md).** (a) *Group at rest.* Let $n$ contestants with missing HP $m_1, \dots, m_n$ stay in range, with no damage. Under [U-0520](../unknowns.md#u-0520) A and B1 the fire restores exactly $\min(P, \sum m_i)$ HP within $P \le 40$ s, whatever the order in which a nearly empty pool serves people. Under B2 the pool is empty after exactly $P$ s and restores $\sum_i \min(m_i, P/n)$ HP, which can be less than $\min(P, \sum m_i)$. Under C the pool is empty after exactly $\lceil P/n \rceil$ s whatever the deficits and restores at most $\min(P, \sum m_i)$; a lone owner at full HP wastes all of it.

(b) *Standby against a steady drain.* A lone contestant at full HP stands by the fire and is hit for $a$ HP at seconds $s, s + b, s + 2b, \dots$ ($1 \le s$, $a \le b$), at most $N$ times ($N \ge 1$, or no limit). Let $K = \lceil P/a \rceil$. Under [U-0520](../unknowns.md#u-0520) A (and B1, which is the same for one person), HP never falls below $M - a$ while the pool lasts, and:

- if $N \ge K$ (or no limit), the pool pays its last HP at second $T_{\text{end}} = s + (K - 1)(b - a) + P - 1$;
- if $N < K$, the pool pays $Na$ HP in all, the last at second $T_{\text{end}} = s + (N - 1)b + a - 1$, and the fire burns out at 600 s with $P - Na$ HP left;

provided $T_{\text{end}} \le 600$; otherwise the fire burns out at 600 s with more HP left. Under B2 and C the pool is empty at $P$ s whatever happens.

*Proof.* (a) Under A and B1 each second moves at least one HP from the pool to a contestant who is missing HP, and only to such a contestant, so the transfer stops at $\min(P, \sum m_i)$, after at most $P$ seconds, long before the 600 s limit. Under B2 the pool loses 1 HP per second and each person receives $1/n$ of it per second until full, so person $i$ receives $\min(m_i, P/n)$ over the $P$ seconds; the sum is at most $\min(P, \sum m_i)$, with equality only when no share is lost. Under C the pool loses $n$ HP per second (the last second possibly fewer), so it lasts $\lceil P/n \rceil$ s; only draws by a contestant missing HP restore anything. (b) Since $a \le b$, each hit is refilled 1 HP per second, starting the second it lands, before the next one lands, so HP returns to $M$ between hits and the pool pays exactly the damage taken. If $N \ge K$, the $K$-th hit is the first after which the pool cannot refill fully; it lands at $s + (K - 1)b$ with $P - (K - 1)a$ HP left, paid one per second. If $N < K$, the last hit lands at $s + (N - 1)b$ and is refilled in $a$ seconds; then nothing draws. Under B2 and C a lone contestant draws 1 HP every second regardless. $\square$

*Examples (gigantic potion, $P = 40$, reading A).* Outside the zone (1 HP every 2 s, [M-0101](../rules/zone.md#m-0101), fire lit when the contestant leaves): first tick at 5 s ([M-0101](../rules/zone.md#m-0101) A) or 7 s (B), so the fire refills every tick and pays its last HP at 83 s or 85 s, against 40 s of burning under B2 or C. Next to one zombie (1 HP every 10 s, [M-0441](../rules/zombies.md#m-0441), phase $s$ from 1 to 10 s, at most 30 HP per zombie and contestant, `wiki-zombies-003` in [M-0635](../rules/zombies.md#m-0635)): the zombie stops at 30 HP, the pool pays its last HP at $s + 290$ s and burns out at 600 s with 10 HP left. A guard zombie (2 HP every 5 s, $s$ from 1 to 5 s, at most 30 HP per contestant, [M-0449](../rules/zombies.md#m-0449)): $s + 71$ s, 10 HP left. Without these caps the times would be $s + 390$ s and $s + 96$ s, pool empty; how the caps count is [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008). Poison (1 HP every 90 s, [M-0170](../rules/poison.md#m-0170)) would need 3,600 s, so the fire burns out at 600 s with 34 HP unused if the first tick is at 90 s ([M-0170](../rules/poison.md#m-0170) A) and 33 HP if it is at 0 s ([M-0170](../rules/poison.md#m-0170) B, the tick at 0 s landing before the fire's first heal).

*Consequence.* Reading A makes the Campfire a 10-minute reserve that absorbs damage as it comes; readings B2 and C make it a burst that is partly or wholly wasted on anyone at full HP nearby. The Ingenuity owner earns one blood token per HP the fire heals ([M-0521](../rules/economy.md#m-0521)); under [U-0519](../unknowns.md#u-0519) A that is the HP restored, under [U-0519](../unknowns.md#u-0519) B the pool consumed, and the two differ only when HP is wasted (readings B2 and C). Whether the fire's healing pays Piety tokens is [U-0502](../unknowns.md#u-0502).

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0520](../unknowns.md#u-0520) (each part names its reading, B split into B1 and B2 as above), on the per-second model with the inclusive 600 s limit and the hit first in a shared second, on everyone staying in range, and for the examples on the [M-0101](../rules/zone.md#m-0101) and [M-0170](../rules/poison.md#m-0170) reading named and on [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008). *Test:* `test_t0304_campfire_pool` (every tier; groups of 1 to 3 with deficits up to 45; every tie order; drains with $a \le 3$, $b$ up to 30, three phases per $b$, hit limits $1$, $K - 1$, $K$ and none; every example).
