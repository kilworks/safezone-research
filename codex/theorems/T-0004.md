**Theorem [T-0004](../theorems/T-0004.md).** With the documented order of step 4, the minimum step ([M-0123](../rules/damage.md#m-0123), "A hit that gets through always does at least 1 HP") changes the step-5 output (not necessarily the final HP loss, since fortress and armor come after) of a raw hit $d \ge 1$ exactly in these cases:

| Defender state | Reading A | Reading B |
|---|---|---|
| live on Kick | $d = 1$ | never |
| Protection, nuke, offline | $d = 1$ | $d = 1$ |
| Protection, nuke, live, two successive floors ([U-0100](../unknowns.md#u-0100) reading A) | $d \in \{1,2,3\}$ | $d = 1$ |
| Protection, nuke, live, one final floor $\lfloor 9d/20 \rfloor$ ([U-0100](../unknowns.md#u-0100) reading B) | $d \in \{1,2\}$ | not defined by [U-0100](../unknowns.md#u-0100) B |
| any other | never | never |

*Proof.* The minimum changes the outcome iff the reduced value is 0 while $d \ge 1$. $R_A(d) = 0 \iff 9d < 10 \iff d = 1$; $R_B(d) = d - \lfloor d/10 \rfloor \ge 1$ for $d \ge 1$; $H(d) = 0 \iff d = 1$; $R_A(H(d)) = 0 \iff H(d) \le 1 \iff d \le 3$; with one final floor, $\lfloor 9d/20 \rfloor = 0 \iff 9d < 20 \iff d \le 2$; $R_B(H(d)) = 0 \iff H(d) = 0 \iff d = 1$. The attacker's Kick bonus (step 2) leaves every row unchanged: $\lfloor 11d/10 \rfloor = d$ for $d \le 9$, and every row only involves $d \le 3$. $\square$
*Consequence.* The minimum step matters only for hits of 3 HP or less. No documented attack deals that little except the 1 HP ticks (zone, unsafe circle, robot, poison, bleeding, airstrike fire, zombie drain) and the 2 HP ticks (guard zombies, tear gas), plus Wizard staff ticks if a staff area ticks ([U-0010](../unknowns.md#u-0010)). Whether those ticks are "hits" that go through the pipeline is [U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002). Whether fortress and armor can still absorb that last HP is [U-0103](../unknowns.md#u-0103).
*Label:* `DERIVED` for each row as stated; which row applies is `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0100](../unknowns.md#u-0100), which column on [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001), the minimum acting at step 5 on [M-0123](../rules/damage.md#m-0123) reading A (reading B puts it after steps 6 and 7), and the in-game relevance on [U-0002](../unknowns.md#u-0002). *Test:* `test_t0004_minimum_step_changes_outcome_only_for_tiny_hits`.
