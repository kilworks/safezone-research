- No game vocabulary in any of the 30 files, except `safezone` (2 hits: a route literal and the search-index path, see RE-0003) and `cost` (1 hit: a field of a Kick channel-reward schema in `cxBucksBalance.DPqxneyE.js`, not a game price).

- The navigation props list a page that the sitemap does not: `/safezone/wiki/chests`. See RE-0004.
