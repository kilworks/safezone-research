# T-0407

Extracted from [theory/04-probabilistic-items.md](../../theory/04-probabilistic-items.md), where the full context, the proof and the test are.

**Theorem [T-0407](../theorems/T-0407.md).** Assume each roll is an independent trial.

(a) Potion break, one roll per drain tick ([U-0426](../unknowns.md#u-0426) reading A): the number of ticks until the first break is geometric with $p = 1/10$: mean 10 ticks (100 s next to one regular zombie), variance 90. Over the 30 ticks a single zombie can drain ([U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) reading A), no potion breaks with probability $(9/10)^{30} \approx 0.0424$. A contestant holding $k$ health potions loses $\min(k, B)$ of them, $B \sim \mathrm{Binomial}(30, 1/10)$: expected $2.9638$ for a Piety stack of $k = 6$ ([M-0506](../rules/items.md#m-0506)), $2.3626$ for $k = 3$. Under reading B (one roll per zombie and contestant) a zombie breaks a potion with probability $1/10$ in total.

(b) Craft destruction, every craft rolls on every hit ([U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) reading A, [U-0427](../unknowns.md#u-0427) reading C): each craft lasts a geometric number of hits with $p = 2/5$: mean $5/2$ hits (25 s next to one regular zombie, 12.5 s next to a guard zombie), variance $15/4$. A craft survives 10 hits with probability $(3/5)^{10} \approx 0.0060$ and a full 30-hit encounter with probability $(3/5)^{30} \approx 2.2 \times 10^{-7}$.
One roll per hit destroying one random craft ([U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) reading B, [U-0427](../unknowns.md#u-0427) reading A): with $c$ crafts, $\min(c, B')$ are destroyed, $B' \sim \mathrm{Binomial}(n, 2/5)$ after $n$ hits: for $c = 5$, an expected $3.7649$ after 10 hits and $4.9981$ after 30; at least one of the five survives 30 hits with probability $\Pr(B' < 5) \approx 0.0015$.
One roll per encounter ([U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) reading C) has two forms, and the wiki does not say which. If the one roll destroys one random craft ([U-0427](../unknowns.md#u-0427) reading B), a given craft among $c$ held survives an encounter with probability $1 - (2/5)/c$ ($23/25$ for $c = 5$), and some craft is lost with probability $2/5$. If instead each craft rolls once per encounter (a per-craft form of [U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) reading C, not one of the readings of [U-0427](../unknowns.md#u-0427)), each survives with probability $3/5$.

Scope of every line above. Under [U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) reading D (a hit blocked by a Shield, repellent or bubble does not roll) and [U-0426](../unknowns.md#u-0426) reading C (a potion roll only when HP is actually lost), the number of rolls $n$ is the number of hits that get through, not the number of ticks; the formulas hold with that $n$. Under [U-0616](../unknowns.md#u-0616) reading A a Necromancy contestant makes no rolls at all. Which items are "crafted items" (all crafts, or only Ingenuity unique crafts) is contradiction [C-0404](../unknowns.md#c-0404); the formulas count whichever items are in scope.

*Proof.* Independent trials with probability $p$: the waiting time to the first success is geometric, mean $1/p$, variance $(1-p)/p^2$; the number of successes in $n$ trials is binomial; a roll that would remove an item when none is left removes nothing, hence the minimum. One roll destroying one of $c$ crafts chosen uniformly hits a given craft with probability $p/c$. Ticks are 10 s apart for a regular zombie and 5 s for a guard zombie. The values are exact fractions computed in the test. $\square$

*Consequence.* Under any per-hit reading, crafts do not survive sustained zombie contact; under the per-encounter readings they usually do. Which one holds changes the value of every Ingenuity craft, and a single VOD of an Ingenuity contestant near a zombie decides it ([U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518)).

*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0426](../unknowns.md#u-0426) or [U-0427](../unknowns.md#u-0427) and [U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) (reading as stated in each line), [U-0518](../unknowns.md#u-0518) reading D and [U-0426](../unknowns.md#u-0426) reading C for what counts as a roll, [U-0616](../unknowns.md#u-0616) (Necromancy), [C-0404](../unknowns.md#c-0404) (scope of "crafted items"), [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) reading A for the 30-tick encounter, [U-0425](../unknowns.md#u-0425) for the tick timing, Q4.1 (independent rolls) and Q4.3 (the 30-tick figures assume rolls stop once the zombie has drained its 30 HP cap from that contestant). The wiki does not say which craft a single roll destroys; the uniform choice behind $1 - (2/5)/c$ is a modelling choice (the counts $\min(c, B')$ do not depend on it). *Test:* `test_t0407_zombie_rolls`.
