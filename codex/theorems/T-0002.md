**Theorem [T-0002](../theorems/T-0002.md).** Let $R_A(d) = \lfloor 9d/10 \rfloor$ (the result is rounded down) and $R_B(d) = d - \lfloor d/10 \rfloor$ (the removed 10% is rounded down). For every integer $d \ge 0$:

$$
R_A(d) = d - \lceil d/10 \rceil, \qquad R_B(d) - R_A(d) = \mathbb{1}[10 \nmid d].
$$

*Rule:* [M-0122](../rules/damage.md#m-0122) (pipeline step 4b), quote "Being live on Kick then takes off 10%, rounded down."; open question [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001).
*Proof.* $\lfloor d - d/10 \rfloor = d + \lfloor -d/10 \rfloor = d - \lceil d/10 \rceil$ by the lemma. Then $R_B - R_A = \lceil d/10 \rceil - \lfloor d/10 \rfloor$, which is 0 when $10 \mid d$ and 1 otherwise. $\square$
*Consequence.* Under A, every hit from 2 to 10 HP loses exactly 1 HP and a 1 HP hit is reduced to 0 (then raised back to 1 by the minimum step, [T-0004](../theorems/T-0004.md)). Under B, hits below 10 HP are not reduced at all. So "you take 10% less damage" is worth either about 1 HP on every small hit or nothing on small hits. Zone ticks of 1 HP are unchanged under both readings.
*Label of the theorem:* `DERIVED`. *Label of the in-game behavior:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0001](../unknowns.md#u-0001). *Test:* `test_t0002_kick_defense_two_readings`.
