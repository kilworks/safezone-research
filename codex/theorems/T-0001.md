**Theorem [T-0001](../theorems/T-0001.md).** For every integer $d \ge 0$: $\lfloor 11d/10 \rfloor = d + \lfloor d/10 \rfloor$.

*Rule:* [M-0119](../rules/damage.md#m-0119) (pipeline step 2), quote "deal 10% more, rounded down".
*Proof.* $11d/10 = d + d/10$ and $d$ is an integer, so the lemma gives the result. $\square$
*Consequence.* "10% more, rounded down" means the same whether the total or the bonus is rounded. The bonus is $\lfloor d/10 \rfloor$, so it adds nothing to hits of 9 HP or less: a live attacker's bow (5 HP), knife (5 HP), fake chest (5 HP) and Zarok's axe at full health (5 HP) do exactly what an offline attacker's do. A 10 HP hit becomes 11, a 20 HP hit 22. These are base values: an enchanted weapon deals +10 ([M-0338](../rules/weapons.md#m-0338)), so an enchanted bow hits for 15 and a live attacker's for 16.
*Label:* `DERIVED`. *Test:* `test_t0001_kick_attack_single_reading`, every $d$ in $0..10000$.
