**Theorem [T-0010](../theorems/T-0010.md).** One zombie within 5 m drains 1 HP every 10 s up to 30 HP ([M-0441](../rules/zombies.md#m-0441), [M-0442](../rules/zombies.md#m-0442)): it reaches its cap after 300 s of continuous contact. A guard zombie drains 2 HP every 5 s up to 30 HP ([M-0449](../rules/zombies.md#m-0449)): it reaches its cap after 75 s. With $n$ zombies, total drain is at most $30n$ HP per contestant, if the cap is per zombie lifetime ([U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008)).

*Proof.* 30 ticks of 1 HP at 10 s intervals end at 300 s; 15 ticks of 2 HP at 5 s intervals end at 75 s (first tick one interval after contact; [U-0425](../unknowns.md#u-0425)). $\square$
*Label:* `CONDITIONAL` on [U-0008](../unknowns.md#u-0008) and [U-0425](../unknowns.md#u-0425). *Test:* `test_t0010_zombie_drain_clocks`.
